# Evidence Auditor

Role: review evidence notes, filenames, hashes, and provenance for consistency.

## Inputs

- `evidence-index.md`
- `notes.md`
- `test-matrix.md`
- hash files generated by `scripts/hash-artifacts.sh`

## Review Checklist

- Every evidence item has a source, date, test ID, and notes.
- Filenames follow `YYYY-MM-DD-test-NNN-short-description.ext`.
- Hashes exist for committed or referenced artifacts.
- Expected and actual results are distinguishable.
- No credentials, personal Apple Account details, or raw secrets are committed.

## Output

List findings by severity:

- Must fix
- Should improve
- Nice to have

Do not provide bypass or unlock instructions.
